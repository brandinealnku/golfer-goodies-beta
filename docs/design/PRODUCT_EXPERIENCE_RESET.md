# Product Experience Reset

## Journey and navigation

The primary journey is Discover → participating Course → Active Round → Product detail → Cart → local demo order → Tracking. External discovered courses show request and map actions but never establish marketplace context. Desktop navigation exposes Course/Discover, Orders, Account, and Cart; mobile adds a four-item bottom bar and contextual floating cart. Placeholder routes are excluded.

## Screen behavior

Discovery offers manual search at all times and requests a nearby lookup only after activation. Storefronts remain browsable before verification and visibly communicate closed or paused ordering. Verification progressively reveals one of location simulation, demo QR, or demo code. Product intent is restored after verification. Checkout collects fulfillment-related details and a display name, never payment credentials. Tracking varies pickup and delivery timelines and labels its controls as local simulation.

## Images and copy

Repository-owned SVG illustrations live under `apps/web/public/images/demo/`. Meaningful course and product images have model-provided alt text; discovery thumbnails are decorative. Consumer copy leads; development limitations remain short and contextual.

## Limitations

All products, courses, rounds, carts, orders, timing, taxes, fees, and progress are fictional. No authentication, real payment, staff workflow, notification, or order submission exists.
